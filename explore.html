Lessons

Prototype

This is a simplified guide to get you playing quickly. Real Phin technique varies by tradition.

  • Phin tunings vary. For this prototype, the strings are labeled E, A, F, G for quick ear training.
  • If you have a real phin, use a tuner app and match your preferred tuning. Consistency matters more than exact pitch at the start.

  • Sit up straight; rest the body of the phin on your thigh. Keep shoulders relaxed.
  • Left hand supports the neck lightly; thumb behind the neck, fingers curved.
  • Right hand hovers over the strings. Use a pick or thumbnail; let the wrist stay loose.

  • Pluck: Aim for a clean, single string stroke. Start slow, keep volume even.
  • Alternate: Practice down up down up on one string for 4 counts.
  • Light strum: Brush two or more strings with a relaxed wrist for texture.
Try on the prototype: Click strings with the mouse or press E, A, F, G on your keyboard.

Press close to the fret wire (not on top) with your fingertip. Keep fingers curved; use minimal pressure for a clean tone. Practice lifting and placing steadily without squeezing.

Count out loud: 1 - 2 - 3 - 4. On each count, pluck a string:
E - A - F - G
Repeat for 4 bars, keeping your volume even. Then reverse: G - F - A - E.

Play two beats, leave two beats. Example:
E - A - (rest) - (rest)
Answer with:
F - G - (rest) - (rest)
This helps timing and phrasing.

  1. 1 min: posture + relaxed hands check
  2. 4 mins: pluck on one string (down up down up), slow and even
  3. 4 mins: 4 beat groove: E - A - F - G, then reverse
  4. 3 mins: call and response patterns with rests
Quick reference
  • Strings in prototype: E • A • F • G
  • Mouse: click to pluck, drag to light strum
  • Keyboard: press E, A, F, G
  • Keep wrists loose; breathe and count out loud
